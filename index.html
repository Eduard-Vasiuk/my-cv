01 / The recruiter question

What happens when the numbers look active, but the signal is wrong?

A campaign can generate clicks and installs while the team still cannot answer what to change next. That is the gap I learn to close.

Problem 01

Installs arrive, but Google Ads sees the wrong event.

Tracking and conversion mapping need to follow the user from the ad click to the meaningful in-app action.

Problem 02

Reports contain numbers, but hide the decision.

Recurring reporting should preserve source truth, surface CPA and ROAS, and make comparison easier.

Problem 03

Support work repeats, so the team loses time.

When a workflow is repetitive, a safe assistant can prepare the next action while keeping human approval in control.

03 / How I work

From messy signal to a useful next step.

My projects follow the same working rhythm: understand the failure, connect the data, then make the result usable for the team.

01 / Diagnose

Find where the story breaks.

Check the campaign goal, event source, attribution path, report fields and data limitations before recommending a change.

02 / Connect

Build the handoff.

Configure tracking parameters, OneLink or conversion mapping so the right signal can travel through the system.

03 / Make usable

Turn evidence into action.

Deliver a campaign draft, a repeatable report, a QA checklist or a safe workflow that another person can use.
